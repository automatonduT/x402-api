# Copy-paste clients, quickstart, playground: integrate in under 5 minutes

The distribution series so far covered *discovery* ([blog10](/blog10)) and *measurement* ([blog9](/blog9)). This one closes the loop on *adoption*:

- **[/playground.html](/playground.html)** - every GET tool as a form, generated from [openapi.json](/openapi.json) at runtime. Never stale.
- **[/quickstart.html](/quickstart.html)** - from machine index to integrated agent in 6 steps.
- **[/examples/client.py](/examples/client.py)** and **[client.js](/examples/client.js)** - stdlib-only Python and zero-dep Node consumers you can paste into your agent today.
- **[/faq.md](/faq.md)** - rate limits, privacy, attribution policy in plain text.
- **[/CHANGELOG.md](/CHANGELOG.md)** - full version history for machine trust checks.

Why ship all five? Because an agent's integration cost is the real price of your API, not your pricing table. Every removed step of friction compounds into recurring usage - which is exactly what our self-reporting embeds then measure back to us ([blog9](/blog9)).
